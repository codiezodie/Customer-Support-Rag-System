--- Policy: Customer Account Management ---

Account Creation:
Creating an account is simple and free. You'll need:
- Valid email address
- Strong password (minimum 8 characters)
- Name and company information
- Verification through email confirmation

Account Security:
We take security seriously. Your account is protected by:
- Encrypted password storage (SHA-256)
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) available
- Session timeout after 30 minutes of inactivity
- Login notifications for new devices
- IP-based suspicious activity detection

Password Management:
Password requirements:
- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

To reset your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email for reset link (valid for 1 hour)
4. Create new password following requirements

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):
Enable 2FA for enhanced security:
1. Go to Account Settings > Security
2. Click "Enable 2FA"
3. Scan QR code with authenticator app
4. Enter verification code
5. Save backup codes in secure location

Profile Management:
You can update the following information:
- Display name and profile picture
- Email address (requires verification)
- Phone number
- Company information
- Notification preferences
- Language and timezone settings

Account Deletion:
To delete your account:
1. Navigate to Settings > Account
2. Click "Delete Account"
3. Confirm deletion via email
4. All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
5. Active subscriptions will be canceled with prorated refunds

Privacy Settings:
Control your data visibility:
- Profile visibility (public/private)
- Activity status
- Email notifications
- Marketing communications
- Data sharing preferences

Account Types:
- Free: Basic features, limited storage
- Professional: Advanced features, priority support
- Enterprise: Custom solutions, dedicated support
