--- Policy: Billing and Subscriptions ---

Billing Overview:
Our billing system operates on a subscription-based model with flexible payment options. All subscriptions are billed monthly or annually based on your chosen plan.

Payment Methods:
We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
- Debit Cards
- PayPal
- Bank Transfer (for enterprise plans)

Billing Cycle:
- Monthly plans: Billed on the same date each month
- Annual plans: Billed once per year with a 20% discount
- Pro-rated charges apply for mid-cycle upgrades

Refund Policy:
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions. To request a refund:
1. Contact support within 30 days of initial purchase
2. Provide your account email and reason for refund
3. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days

Subscription Management:
You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings.
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle
- Cancellations can be processed anytime, with access until the end of the paid period

Late Payments:
If a payment fails:
- We'll retry the payment 3 times over 10 days
- Your account will be suspended after failed attempts
- A $10 late fee may apply
- Reactivation requires payment of outstanding balance

Enterprise Billing:
Enterprise customers have custom billing options including:
- Quarterly or annual invoicing
- Purchase order processing
- Net-30 payment terms
- Dedicated account manager

Tax Information:
Applicable taxes are calculated based on your billing address. EU customers may be subject to VAT. Tax exemption certificates can be uploaded in account settings.
